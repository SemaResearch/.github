Every trading idea sounds convincing until you test it. SemaResearch puts popular market claims on trial with real data: famous chart setups, seasonality, the VIX buy signal, earnings drift, sector rotation, insider buying, and more.

Each study follows one question from start to finish. We state the claim, build the test, compare it against random entries, check it out of sample, and report what survived and what didn't, including the results that fail.

**How we work**
- Swing horizons on daily bars, with simple rules a person could follow.
- Survivorship-bias-free, point-in-time data.
- Every edge is compared with random entries and confirmed out of sample.
- Failed ideas are published too.
